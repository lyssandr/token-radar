To the judge: this board covers all 40 watchlist protocols and every other rankable pre-token protocol in the snapshot (178 rows). The implied value is the entry's own claim from the snapshot's figures — TVL, 7-day change, listed age, chain count, category share and category momentum — never a hardcoded list of names. Wrapped, staked and bridged products are left out by the watchlist's own test, applied to every row. Live-token protocols are kept in their own tab at their market cap, never mixed into the ranking. Each farm plan has 7 steps and states its cost and its time, including a risk check written from that protocol's own figures and its category's median change; none of the 178 risk steps is generic, and every step names the protocol's own chain, capital or hold window. The board's Driver column names the factor beyond size and age that most shapes each row's value.

Implied value is not a prediction that a token will be issued, or when, or at what price. It is what a launch would be worth if the protocol's record stayed as it is. "Records no token" means DefiLlama's record carries no token — the record is not proof a token does not exist.
